What are the drawbacks of impact windows?
Impact windows carry a higher upfront investment than standard windows, and their heavy laminated glass requires proper structural support and skilled installation to avoid gaps or seal failures. If the glass does crack under severe force, the full unit typically needs replacing rather than a simple patch. Still, most homeowners find the added protection and energy efficiency worth these tradeoffs. LockTight Impact Windows can walk you through what fits your home.
What is the 25% rule for windows in Florida?
The 25% rule refers to a Florida building code provision stating that once you replace more than a quarter of your home's total glass surface area in a 12 month span, every new window in that project must meet current hurricane impact standards. Replacing less than that threshold still requires basic code compliance, just not a full upgrade. LockTight Impact Windows can evaluate your home's glass area and confirm what your project requires.
What are the best brands of impact windows for Florida?
The best choice depends on your budget, frame material preference, and how the windows will hold up to Florida's testing and coastal exposure, since certified brands vary in glass type, frame strength, and energy performance. Rather than favor one label, LockTight Impact Windows, Doors, & Roofing evaluates your home's wind zone, style needs, and budget, then recommends products that meet Florida's impact and building code requirements for lasting protection.
Can I get tax credit for impact windows in Florida?
Availability varies, since federal, state, and local incentive programs change from year to year and depend on your home's value, location, and current funding cycles. Some homeowners may qualify for state-level sales tax relief or wind mitigation savings tied to certified impact products, while insurance premium discounts are also common. LockTight Impact Windows can help verify which products qualify and provide documentation for any program you pursue.
Do impact windows lower homeowners insurance?
Yes, in most cases impact windows can reduce the windstorm portion of a homeowners policy, since insurers view them as lowering storm damage risk. The actual savings varies by insurer, location, and whether every opening on the home, not just some windows, is protected. A wind mitigation inspection is typically required to document the upgrade. LockTight Impact Windows, Doors, & Roofing can advise which products meet local qualifying standards.
Can a burglar break impact windows?
Yes, with enough time and force a determined burglar can crack impact glass, but the laminated inner layer keeps it bonded to the frame instead of shattering, so there is no quick opening to climb through. This delay is the main deterrent, often forcing intruders to give up or move on before breaking through.
